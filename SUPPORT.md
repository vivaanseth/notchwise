# Support

Notchwise is a personal, free and open source project looked after on a best
effort basis. The [troubleshooting guide](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) covers the
usual fixes.

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an [issue](https://github.com/vivaanseth/notchwise/issues). Include your Notchwise and
  macOS versions and clear steps to reproduce.
- **Security issues:** see the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
